# Code of Conduct

auto-gallery welcomes contributions from people of different backgrounds,
skill levels, and viewpoints. Keep discussions constructive, specific, and
respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Critique code, behavior, and tradeoffs rather than people.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on actionable project work.
- Respect privacy. Do not post private cookies, account data, creator archives,
  or other sensitive material.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Repeated off-topic arguing after maintainers ask to refocus
- Encouraging illegal downloads, credential abuse, or bypassing access controls

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict
participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
