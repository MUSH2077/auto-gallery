- Batch slices process at most 25 sources or 20 seconds, deferring transient admission failures 30 seconds to 5 minutes without occupying a worker while waiting. Persist a unique batch/source item and bind its download dispatch in the same transaction.
- Preserve all three sync modes. manual_all_enabled includes manual subscriptions but still checks enabled status, ownership, credentials and provider download capability. force_eligible and due_scan retain their narrower eligibility.
- Final results distinguish queued, waiting, downloading, importing, succeeded, skipped and failed. Partial failures never produce an all-success notification. Duplicate submission and restart recovery must not duplicate downloads. Cancellation stops pending publication and safely cancels owned unfinished children, preserving completed results.
- Global sync execution, viewing and control require admin/system privileges; private actions keep their membership boundaries. Repair the known legacy batch by reconciling receipts/current jobs before scheduling its still-unprocessed sources.
- Audit all 52 route files and shared controls, including modal/menu/mobile actions. Validate real state changes, not only a fulfilled HTTP mock or a toast. Chinese/English, desktop/mobile, admin/restricted, empty/pending/error states are in scope.
- Keep layout/functionality; change only necessary copy, state, loading and action behavior. Destructive actions are tested only on isolated fixtures. No missing actions, unexplained skips or unresolved functional defects may be marked passed.
- Retain the old production observation and annotate the known fault in a new artifact; start a new version-specific observation only after a successful reviewed rollout.
